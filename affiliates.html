We offer a standard 30-day refund window as required by Digistore24. Our product is built to deliver real value, so refund rates are low. If a refund does occur, the commission is automatically reversed in Digistore24's system.

Paid traffic (Facebook Ads, Google Ads, native) requires prior written approval. We want to review your ad copy and angles to make sure they're compliant and on-brand. Reach out before launching any paid campaigns.

No minimums. Whether you have a 200-person email list or 200k followers, you're welcome to promote. We love working with builders at every stage.
How long does approval take?+
Usually within 24 hours — often same-day during business hours. You'll receive an email with your affiliate link and the full swipe file vault link once approved.
