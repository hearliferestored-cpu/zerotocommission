Wherever you post, three rules don't change

  1. Disclose, every time, before the click. Say you earn a commission in plain words, where people will see it. Our disclosure generator writes the wording for each platform.
  2. Follow the program's terms too. A platform allowing a link doesn't mean the program allows it there. Some ban certain traffic sources, ads on their brand name, or links in emails.
  3. Don't hide where a link goes. Shorteners and redirects that disguise the destination break some platforms' rules (Pinterest) and some programs' terms (Amazon).

No clickable link where you post? A link-in-bio page or a short bridge page is the usual answer. See affiliate marketing without a website.

Questions

Why aren't links in my TikTok bio clickable?

TikTok only shows a clickable website link for accounts with at least 1,000 followers, or a Verified Business Account.

Can I put affiliate links in YouTube Shorts?

You can type them, but since August 2023 links in Shorts descriptions and comments aren't clickable. Channel profile links and long-form video descriptions still are.

Can I put Amazon affiliate links in emails?

No. Amazon Associates doesn't allow its special links in emails or offline documents. Send readers to a page of your own instead.
