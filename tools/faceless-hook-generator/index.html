How to use a hook without overpromising

  1. The video has to deliver what the hook promises. If the hook says "3 things", show three things. A hook the video doesn't pay off loses trust fast.
  2. Say it in the first second, and put it on screen. Many people watch without sound, so use the short on-screen version as your first caption.
  3. Keep results out of it. No income figures, "guaranteed", "life-changing" or before-and-after claims, about money or health.
  4. Mention a product only when it actually helps. Most of your videos should simply be useful. When you do mention one, disclose it. Our disclosure generator writes the wording.

Questions

What makes a good hook for a faceless Short?

It names who the video is for and the problem in a few words, and it promises something the video can actually show. Specific beats clever.

Do these hooks work on TikTok, Reels and Shorts?

They're plain-language openings, so they fit any short-form platform. How well any video does depends on the topic, the content and luck; no hook guarantees views.
