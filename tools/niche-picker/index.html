How the score works

Each "yes" scores 2 points, "not sure" scores 1 and "no" scores 0, for a total out of 10. The questions come from our guide to the best niches for affiliate marketing for beginners:

  1. Ideas: could you make 30 short videos about it without running out of ideas?
  2. Demand: do people already ask questions about it? Type the topic into TikTok, YouTube or Google search and look at the suggestions.
  3. Programs: is there at least one free affiliate program with honest, beginner-friendly products? See free affiliate programs.
  4. Faceless: can you make content about it without showing your face, if you want to?
  5. Low harm: if someone followed your advice and it went wrong, would the harm be small?

The tool also looks for words in your topic that suggest health, weight loss, relationships, making money online, or areas that need professional qualifications (medical, legal, investing). Those get a warning however you answer. The word check is simple, so read your own topic honestly too.

What this tool can't tell you

  • How much you'll earn. Nobody can honestly predict that, and many people who try affiliate marketing earn nothing.
  • How crowded a niche is. Almost every topic has competition. A narrower angle, such as "budgeting for nursing students", is easier to stand out in.
  • Whether a specific product is good. Check each offer with our 10-question checklist before you promote it.

Questions

What's the best niche for affiliate marketing for a beginner?

One you can keep making content about for months, where people already look for help and honest products exist. Productivity, hobbies and skills, software tools and everyday budgeting basics often fit. The right one for you depends on your answers, which is what this tool is for.

What if two topics get the same score?

Pick the one you'd still enjoy talking about in six months. You can narrow it or switch after a few weeks of posting, once you see what people actually watch.

Should I avoid a niche the tool flags?

"Extra care" doesn't mean never. It means checking every offer strictly and making no claims you can't back up. "Avoid for now" is for topics that need professional qualifications to advise on, which we wouldn't recommend to a beginner.

Do you save what I type?

No. The niche picker runs entirely in your browser.
