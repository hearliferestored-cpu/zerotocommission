Before you publish it

  1. Read every line out loud. If something isn't true for you, change it or delete it. The draft is a starting point, not a claim you can make by default.
  2. Keep the disclosure right under the button. Not in the footer, not behind a link.
  3. Answer the FAQs from the seller's own pages. Don't guess at refund terms or what's included.
  4. Check the program's terms. Some vendors have rules about bridge pages, wording or traffic sources.
  5. Add nothing about results. No income figures, no "guaranteed", no before-and-after promises.

Where to put it: any free one-page builder or link-in-bio tool works. See Affiliate Marketing Without a Website for the options and their trade-offs.

Questions

Does a bridge page need to be long?

No. One short screen that answers "what is this, who is it for, and what did you check?" does the job. Search engines tend to ignore thin pages, so if you want one to rank, add genuinely useful content of your own.

Can I use AI to write the rest?

You can use it for drafts, but every statement still has to be true and checked by you. See What Is a Bridge Page? for more.

Do you save what I type?

No. The generator runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent to us or stored.
