How the calculator works

FigureFormula
Commission per salePrice x commission %. Or the listing's earnings per sale, if you entered it.
After refundsCommission per sale x (1 - refund rate). An average: each individual sale either stays or is reversed.
Click-to-sale ratioSales / clicks.
EPC (earnings per click)Commission per sale after refunds x sales / clicks.
Sales for your goalGoal / commission after refunds, rounded up.
Clicks for your goalSales for your goal / your click-to-sale ratio. Only shown when you have at least one sale.

Payout fees, taxes and currency conversion aren't included. Check your platform's payout terms; for Digistore24 see the payment section of our Digistore24 guide.

How to use the result

  • Compare offers, don't forecast income. The useful question is "which of these two offers pays more per sale after refunds?", not "how much will I make?".
  • A high commission isn't the whole story. A product that rarely sells or gets refunded a lot can be worth less than a lower commission on a product people keep.
  • Your own ratio needs time. With a few dozen clicks, one sale more or less changes everything. Note your numbers weekly.
  • Then check the offer itself. Numbers can't tell you whether the sales page is honest. Our guide to finding affiliate products to promote has ten questions for that.

Questions

What is a good EPC?

There's no universal number. EPC depends on the product, the price, your audience and where your clicks come from. Use it to compare your own offers and to see whether changes you make help over time.

Is this the same as Digistore24's conversion rate?

No. Digistore24's Conversion rate counts buyers among people who reached the order form. This calculator's click-to-sale ratio counts buyers among everyone who clicked your link, which is always lower.

Do you save what I type?

No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent to us or stored.
