How to Build an Email List for Affiliate Marketing, From Zero

Social platforms decide who sees your posts. An email list is the one audience you can reach directly. Here's how to build one for affiliate marketing from zero, what to send, and the rules that apply, including why Amazon links don't belong in your emails.

Why build an email list for affiliate marketing?

  • You reach people directly. No algorithm decides whether your subscribers see a post.
  • You keep it if a platform changes. If an account is restricted or reach drops, your list stays yours.
  • You can be more useful. An email has room for detail a 30-second video doesn't.

It's slower to grow than a social following, and an email list doesn't guarantee sales. It's a long-term asset, not a shortcut.

What you need

  1. An email service that handles sign-up forms, unsubscribes and sending. Many have a free plan for small lists; check the current limits before you choose.
  2. A reason to subscribe, usually a small free resource (a checklist, a cheat sheet, a short guide) related to your topic.
  3. A sign-up page or form you can link from your profile, bridge page or website.
  4. A short welcome sequence: a few emails that deliver the freebie and introduce what you'll send.

How to build an email list from zero, step by step

1. Pick one small, useful freebie

Make it something your audience can use in five minutes, tied to the problem your content covers. For example, a one-page shopping list for night-shift meal prep. Don't promise results it can't deliver.

2. Set up the form with double opt-in

Double opt-in asks people to confirm their address. It keeps your list clean and shows they really asked to hear from you.

3. Put the link where people already find you

Your profile link, a link-in-bio page or a bridge page. Mention the freebie in videos where it genuinely helps. Our link rules checker shows where links are clickable on each platform.

4. Write a welcome sequence of 3 to 5 emails

Deliver the freebie first. Then send one or two genuinely helpful emails before you recommend anything.

5. Send on a schedule you can keep

Every week or every two weeks is plenty. Consistency matters more than frequency.

What to send

  • Answers to questions your audience asks, in more depth than a video allows
  • Short lists, checklists and tips they can use straight away
  • Honest notes on products, including who they're not for
  • Now and then, a recommendation with an affiliate link and a clear disclosure

If most emails are simply helpful, the occasional recommendation is welcome rather than ignored.

The rules for affiliate links in emails

  • Amazon links aren't allowed in emails. Amazon Associates doesn't permit its special links in emails or attachments. Link to a page of your own that has the Amazon link instead.
  • Check every other program's terms. Most allow links in emails to people who subscribed, but some restrict it.
  • Disclose in the email, near the link, in plain words such as "I earn a commission if you buy through this link."
  • Follow CAN-SPAM in the US: no misleading subject lines or sender names, include your valid physical postal address, offer a clear way to unsubscribe, and honor unsubscribe requests within 10 business days.
  • Get consent first. Only email people who signed up, and never buy lists. Rules are stricter in some places, such as the EU and UK.

Can you do this without showing your face?

Yes. Email is text, so it suits faceless creators well. You can sign emails with a first name or brand name. Being faceless doesn't mean being anonymous, though: CAN-SPAM still requires a valid postal address. Many creators use a PO box or a private mailbox service for this.

Questions beginners ask

How many subscribers do you need for affiliate marketing?

There's no magic number. A small list of people who asked for your help is more useful than a big list that never opens your emails.

Can I put affiliate links directly in emails?

For many programs, yes, with a disclosure. Not for Amazon Associates. Always check each program's terms.

What's a good freebie for an affiliate email list?

Something small and immediately useful for your audience's problem, like a checklist, template or short guide.

Do I need a website to build an email list?

No. Most email services give you a hosted sign-up page you can link from your profile.
