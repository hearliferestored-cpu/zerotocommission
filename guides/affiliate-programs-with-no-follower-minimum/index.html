Affiliate Programs With No Follower Minimum: What Beginners Can Join

Some affiliate programs ask for an audience before they'll approve you. Many don't. Here's how to tell the difference, which kinds of programs usually have no follower minimum, and the ones that do have a bar.

The short answer

Yes, you can join affiliate programs with zero followers. The ones that let you in without an audience are mostly affiliate marketplaces and networks, where you sign up first and get a link without anyone checking your follower count. Programs run by social platforms (TikTok Shop, YouTube Shopping) and some big retailers usually do set a bar.

Requirements change often, and some programs review every application by hand. Treat everything below as a starting point, and read each program's current terms before you rely on it.

Programs that usually have no follower minimum

TypeHow it usually worksWatch out for
Digital product marketplaces (for example ClickBank, Digistore24)Free sign-up, then you pick products and get your link. No audience check at sign-up.Quality varies a lot. Some listings make income or health claims you shouldn't repeat. Some vendors ask you to request approval for their product.
Creator platforms with affiliate features (for example Gumroad)Each creator decides whether to offer affiliates for their products and on what terms.Not every product has an affiliate option, and the creator may approve affiliates one by one.
Affiliate networks (for example Impact, CJ, Awin)You join the network, then apply to each brand separately.Joining the network is the easy part. Each brand can say no, often because it wants to see a site or channel with real content.
Individual brands' own programsMany software and online businesses run their own program with a simple application form.Read who they accept. Some want a website, a minimum audience, or a specific country.

"No follower minimum" isn't the same as "anyone gets approved". Even where there's no number, a reviewer may want to see that you post real, original content.

Programs that do have a bar

Amazon Associates

Amazon lets you sign up first, then reviews your account once you've made three qualifying sales within 180 days (your own orders don't count). For social media pages, Amazon's help page says it expects an established page with a substantive number of organic followers, "in most cases, at least 500". Websites need original, recent content. Details in our Amazon 3-sales rule guide.

Amazon Influencer Program

This is a separate program from Associates, aimed at creators with an existing social following. Amazon doesn't publish a fixed number; it looks at your account when you apply.

TikTok Shop affiliate

TikTok Shop sets a follower minimum for creators who apply on their own, along with age and location rules. Reports from 2026 disagree on the exact US number and it has changed more than once, so check TikTok Shop's current requirements in the app before planning around it.

YouTube Shopping affiliate program

YouTube says the program is open to creators in the YouTube Partner Program with at least 500 subscribers, in supported countries. The Partner Program itself also has watch-time requirements. You can check your own eligibility in the Earn section of YouTube Studio.

How to start with zero followers

  1. Pick one program with no audience check, and inside it, one product you'd be comfortable recommending to a friend. Our 10-question checklist helps.
  2. Make helpful content first. Short videos, pins or posts that answer real questions about the problem the product solves. That's also what reviewers look for when a program does check.
  3. Disclose every time. Use our free disclosure generator for the wording.
  4. Apply to programs with a bar later, once you have content and some real followers to show. Applying with an empty account usually just gets you rejected.

Where your first viewers come from with no followers is covered in affiliate marketing without followers.

Red flags

  • You have to pay to join a "program" or buy a "license" to get your link. Legitimate affiliate programs are free to join.
  • Income promises on the signup page ("earn $500 a day, no followers needed").
  • Pressure to buy followers to qualify somewhere. Bought followers break most platform rules and don't buy anything.

Questions beginners ask

What's the easiest affiliate program to get accepted into?

Marketplaces where you sign up and get a link straight away are the easiest to join. Easy to join isn't the same as easy to earn from, so still check the product first.

Can I join Amazon Associates with no followers?

You can sign up, and you get links straight away. Amazon reviews your account after three qualifying sales in 180 days, and for social pages it says it usually expects at least 500 organic followers.

Do I need a website to join affiliate programs?

Not always. Many programs accept a social media account, and some don't ask for either at sign-up. See affiliate marketing without a website.
