Digistore24 for Beginners: How to Use It as an Affiliate

Digistore24 is a marketplace and payment platform for digital products, and a common starting point for new affiliates. Here's how it works from the affiliate side, how to read a product listing, and what to check before you promote anything.

What is Digistore24?

Digistore24 is a marketplace and payment platform for digital products such as online courses, e-books and software. Vendors list their products there and run checkout through it. Affiliates browse the marketplace, choose products to recommend, and promote them with a personal tracking link. When someone buys through that link, Digistore24 records the sale and calculates the affiliate's commission under the vendor's and the platform's terms.

How to sign up as a Digistore24 affiliate

  1. Go to digistore24.com and choose the option to create a free account.
  2. Follow the prompts and confirm your email address.
  3. Complete your profile. Digistore24 may ask for payout and tax details when you're ready to be paid. Give accurate information and keep it private.
  4. Never share your password, and be wary of anyone who offers to "set up your account for you".

Then take a few minutes just to look around before you choose anything: find the marketplace, see how products are grouped into categories, and open a few listings.

How to read a Digistore24 product listing

A marketplace listing typically shows the product price, the commission rate, and some performance statistics. Depending on the product, these may include:

StatisticWhat it suggestsRule of thumb
Conversion rateRoughly how many visitors to the sales page buy.Many beginners look for around 1% or better, but compare similar products.
Earnings per saleWhat an affiliate receives per sale.Decide what's worth your time; there's no universal cutoff.
Cancellation or refund rateHow many buyers ask for their money back.Be cautious above about 10%. Refunds usually reverse commissions.

Two cautions. These are historical figures from other affiliates, not predictions of what will happen for you. And new products may have very little data, so lean harder on reading the sales page itself.

Example with made-up numbers: a $47 product with a 50% commission pays $23.50 per sale before any refunds. That's arithmetic, not a forecast. Many people who try affiliate marketing earn little or nothing.

How to choose a product to promote

Statistics are only half the picture. Read the sales page as a skeptical buyer would: is the price clear, is there a real seller, is there a refund policy, and are there any guaranteed-income claims or countdown timers that reset? If the page makes claims you couldn't repeat truthfully, skip it, however good the numbers look. Our guide on how to find affiliate products to promote has the full 10-question checklist.

Also read each product's affiliate terms. Vendors may set rules about where you can promote, what you can say, and sometimes whether they need to approve you first.

Getting your link and promoting it

Once you've chosen a product, generate your personal tracking link from its listing. That link is how Digistore24 knows a sale came through you. Then share it where your audience is, for example in your profile on a short-form video platform, directly or through a bridge page.

Disclose it every time, in the video and the caption. See our affiliate disclosure examples. Digistore24's own terms require affiliates to follow the law and the platform's rules too.

Reading your affiliate statistics

Your affiliate account shows how your links perform. Check three things weekly rather than daily, because daily numbers swing a lot:

  • Clicks: if they're low, look at your content and how visible your link is.
  • Conversion rate: sales divided by clicks. If clicks are healthy but sales are rare, check the fit between your content, your audience and the offer.
  • Earnings: track them over weeks, and remember refunds can reverse commissions.

Digistore24 payment methods

"Payment methods" means two different things on Digistore24: how buyers can pay, and how you get paid as an affiliate. Here's both, based on Digistore24's help center as of November 2026. Details depend on your country and which Digistore24 company handles your sales, so check your own account settings.

How buyers can pay

The vendor chooses which payment methods each product's order form offers, from options that include credit card, PayPal, SEPA direct debit, bank transfer/prepayment, Apple Pay and Google Pay, Klarna, Sezzle and instant bank payments. Which ones a buyer actually sees also depends on their country, the currency and the type of payment (one-time, subscription or installments). As an affiliate you don't choose these, but it's worth opening the order form to see what your audience will be offered.

How affiliates get paid

You set this up under Settings > Payout account. There are four payout methods, and which ones you can use depends on the Digistore24 company (Digistore24 calls it the "reseller") that handles your sales:

Payout methodNotes
PayPalAvailable with every reseller. Setup includes a small one-time verification purchase (1.00 in your currency).
US bank transfer (ACH)US reseller only, paid in USD.
SEPA transferEuropean resellers only, paid in EUR.
International bank transferAvailable with every reseller. Your own bank may also charge to receive it.

Wise and Payoneer aren't separate payout methods: they give you bank details that you enter under a bank-transfer method. Digistore24 charges a fee on each payout, which depends on the method and reseller.

When payouts happen

  • Payouts run on up to four dates a month: the 7th, 14th, 21st and 28th. You choose which dates you want.
  • The minimum is 50 (in USD, EUR, GBP or CHF) on the 7th and 200 on the other dates, or 250 on any date for international transfers. You can set a higher threshold.
  • After a payout date it can take up to ten business days for the money to arrive.
  • Refunds can reverse commissions, so not everything you see as a sale ends up paid out.

Source: Digistore24 Help Center, Payout: set method and currency, Payout: set dates and thresholds and Set payment methods, checked November 20, 2026.

Questions beginners ask

Is Digistore24 legit?

Digistore24 is a real, established marketplace used by many vendors and affiliates. But a legitimate platform doesn't mean every product on it is good. Check each product yourself before you put your name on it.

Does it cost anything to join Digistore24 as an affiliate?

Creating an affiliate account is free. Payouts do carry a fee, and there are minimum payout amounts; see Digistore24 payment methods above and check the current terms.

Can I use Digistore24 on TikTok?

Many affiliates share links through short-form video profiles. Follow TikTok's rules on links and disclosure, the product's affiliate terms, and Digistore24's terms. Some vendors restrict certain traffic sources.

Do I need a website for Digistore24?

No. You can share your link through a profile link or a simple bridge page. Check the product's terms for any requirements.
