How to Start Affiliate Marketing With No Money: Step by Step

You can start affiliate marketing without spending money. But "free" has fine print. Here's what really costs nothing, what doesn't, and a step-by-step way to start with an empty budget.

Can you start affiliate marketing with no money?

Yes. Joining many affiliate programs is free, posting on social platforms is free, and you can make simple videos with a phone and free tools. The real cost is your time: learning, making content, and waiting through a slow start. If you need money this month, affiliate marketing is the wrong plan.

How to start affiliate marketing with no money, step by step

  1. Pick a topic you can talk about for months. Something you know or genuinely want to learn, such as productivity, a hobby, a software tool or budgeting basics.
  2. Join one free affiliate program or marketplace that fits that topic. Read its terms, payout rules and any tax or identity requirements.
  3. Choose one offer and check it properly. Use our 10-question checklist. One offer, not ten.
  4. Set up one free account on a short-form platform you already know. A logo instead of your face is fine. See faceless affiliate marketing.
  5. Add your link to your profile, directly or through a free link-in-bio page, with a visible disclosure.
  6. Post short, useful videos about the problem your offer solves: hook, one useful idea, soft call to action. Disclose in every one (examples here).
  7. Check your numbers weekly, not daily, and make more of what people actually watch.

Hidden costs to watch for

  • Tool upgrades. Free tools often nudge you toward paid plans. Only upgrade when a limit is genuinely in your way.
  • Licenses. Using a free voice, song or clip outside its license can get videos removed. That's a cost in time.
  • Payout minimums and fees. Programs often pay out only above a minimum balance, and some payout methods charge fees.
  • Refunds. If a buyer gets a refund, your commission is usually reversed.
  • Taxes. Commissions are generally taxable income. Check the rules where you live.
  • "Systems" that cost money to join. If a program makes you pay to participate or earns from recruiting people, that's not the kind of affiliate marketing this guide describes.

Can you start affiliate marketing with just your phone?

Yes. A phone can handle the account, the screen recordings, the editing and the posting. Many faceless creators never use a computer for their short videos. A computer makes research and longer editing easier, but it isn't required to begin.

Questions beginners ask

Is it really $0 to start?

It can be, if you stick to free accounts, free tool plans used within their licenses, and organic posting. What it does cost is time, and possibly small optional costs later.

How long until I earn my first commission?

Nobody can honestly tell you. Some beginners take months, and many people who try affiliate marketing earn nothing. Treat the first weeks as practice.

Do I need followers first?

No. Short-form platforms can show videos to people who don't follow you. See affiliate marketing without followers.

Should I buy a course before I start?

You don't have to. A course can save time by putting the steps in order, but the basics in our free guides are enough to begin. If you do buy one, avoid any that promise income.
