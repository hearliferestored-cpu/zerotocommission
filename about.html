Who's behind this,
and how we work

Who runs Zero to Commission

Zero to Commission is a one-person business: a sole proprietor in Louisiana, United States, signing emails as Keath. There is no team, no call center and no sales staff. The full business details (owner, mailing address, payment processor) are on the Legal Information page.

You can reach a real person at support@zerotocommission.com, for questions before you buy, help after you buy, or a refund.

The brand is "faceless" because the course teaches faceless content, and the lessons practise what they teach. Faceless does not mean anonymous: the business and the person behind it are named on the Legal page.

How we make money

From one thing: sales of the Zero to Commission course ($47, one time, sold through Gumroad). The free checklist and the free guides are free, and you never need to buy anything to use them.

None of the links on this website are affiliate links. When a guide or the course names a company or platform, nobody paid for that mention. If that ever changes, the link will be clearly disclosed where it appears.

How we use AI

The video lessons are presented by an AI-generated narrator, not a real person telling a personal story. The workbook covers the same lessons in writing.

Our short videos on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook use an AI voice with stock footage, and each one is labeled or described as AI-made on the platform.

Writing: we use AI tools to help draft and check material. A person reviews everything before it is published, and we check every claim against what the course actually contains.

We never use AI to create testimonials, reviews, results or people who don't exist.

Our no-income-claims rule

We don't tell you what you will earn, what other people earned, or how fast. No earnings screenshots, no "passive income," no lifestyle photos, no "results not typical" small print hiding a big promise. Most beginners earn little or nothing at first, and some never earn a commission.

When the course or a guide shows a dollar figure, it is arithmetic (for example, price times commission rate) to explain how payouts work, not a prediction. See the Earnings Disclaimer.

Testimonials: we have none yet and won't invent any. When real reviews come in, we won't pay for positive ones, hide negative ones, or tie a refund to a review. If someone received the course for free in exchange for an honest review, we'll say so next to it.

We only describe what we deliver

Everything this website says the course includes is in the course: five video lessons (about 32 minutes in total, with subtitle files), a 12-page workbook, four bonus PDFs and a copy-paste text file. If something isn't built yet, we don't mention it as if it were.

The refund terms are the same everywhere: 30 days, any reason, full refund, as set out in the Refund Policy.
