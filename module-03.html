Module 03

Your Free Traffic Engine

Get real eyes on your affiliate links without spending a single dollar on ads.

Lesson Overview

This is where the action starts. In Module 03 you'll set up your faceless TikTok or Instagram page from scratch, learn the exact short-form content formula that drives affiliate clicks, and understand how to use your bio link and bridge pages to send traffic to your offer — no website required.

What You'll Learn
◆
Setting up your faceless content account Step-by-step account setup for TikTok or Instagram — brand name, bio, profile image, and link setup. You never go on camera.
◆
The short-form content formula The exact structure every high-performing affiliate video follows — hook, value, soft CTA. You'll use this for every video you post.
◆
Bio links and bridge pages How to turn a single link in your bio into a conversion machine — and why going straight to the sales page is usually the wrong move.
◆
Posting cadence for beginners How often to post, what time, and what format gives you the best chance of early traction without burning out.

Your Action Steps

  • ✓Watch the full module video above

  • ✓Set up your bio link pointing to your first affiliate offer
  • ✓Script your first video using the content formula from this module
