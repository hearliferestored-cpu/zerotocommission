How affiliate marketing works

Affiliate marketing means recommending someone else's product with a special tracking link. If someone buys through your link, the seller pays you a commission. In practice, doing it comes down to four things:

  1. Join an affiliate program and get your link. Legitimate programs are free to join.
  2. Choose one product you can honestly recommend to a specific group of people.
  3. Make helpful content where those people already spend time, such as short faceless videos, and put your link where the platform allows it.
  4. Disclose the link every time, then watch what people respond to and make more of it.

It costs little or no money to start, but it takes time, and many people who try it earn nothing. The steps below go through each part.
